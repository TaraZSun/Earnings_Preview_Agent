**MSFT Q4 2024 preview**

**Setup:** Reports July 30, 2024. Through July 29, shares fell 5.77% over one month but gained 5.02% over three months versus SPY’s 7.18% gain (unadjusted closes, excluding dividends).

**Guidance vs consensus:**
- Revenue: $64.37bn consensus sits inside the $63.50–$64.50bn range **calculated from segment guidance**, above its $64.00bn midpoint by 0.58% and toward the upper bound. This is not explicitly stated consolidated guidance; the midpoint is not management’s forecast. **Read:** Consensus requires delivery toward the range’s upper end.
- EPS: Consensus is $2.93; its GAAP/non-GAAP basis is unspecified. Management supplied no comparable EPS guidance.

**Watch:**
- Azure guidance is 30%–31% constant-currency revenue growth. Q&A confirmed capacity constraints affect infrastructure consumption, not Copilots. **Read:** How much demand can new capacity convert into revenue during the quarter?
- Management expects cloud gross margin down roughly two percentage points year-over-year, but only slightly excluding the useful-life accounting-estimate change; capex should rise materially sequentially. **Read:** Can operating efficiencies offset AI infrastructure costs and Azure mix pressure on profitability?
- Office 365 revenue growth guidance is approximately 14% constant currency, with ARPU growth primarily through E5. **Read:** Does Copilot adoption add measurable revenue beyond premium-suite upgrades?

**Risks:** FX is expected to reduce revenue growth by less than one point. Moderating LinkedIn and Dynamics bookings are explicit revenue headwinds.

**Data gaps:** No matching business-level consensus supplied. Consensus provider and EPS accounting basis unspecified; historical EPS surprise data unavailable.
